## General Changes (bux fixes)
This section pertains to changes to be made across all system interfaces. Contains major bux fix within transaction handling, transaction details, and others.
> 1. In an instance of downpayments, certain bug happens which are the following:
```
Problem:
> when creating a transaction with a downpayment Payment arrangement. The system doesn't have a confirmation to pay the downpayment balance on cash payment mode.
> when creating a transaction with a downpament Payment arrangement with a QrPH payment mode, the system offers a notice to pay the down payment. But once the payment was made, the whole transaction becames paid.
> when creating a transaction with a downpament Payment arrangement with a QrPH payment mode, instead of the stated downpayment balance was to be paid initially. The whole remaining balance was getting paid (e.g. my entire balance is 1,500 pesos, and i chose a downpayment of 500. Instead of paying for 500 pesos initially for my downpayment, the remaining balance of 1,000 pesos was getting paid.)

Solution:
> for cash, after saving the transaction create a notice and a confirmation that will be saved to the transactions, that the client must pay the initial balance (downpayment) first. When paying for remaining balance, the remaining balance only must be paid deducting the initial payment made. After paying the remaining balance only the transaction will be marked as `paid`.
> same as cash, for QrPH after saving the transaction a qr for the downpayment balance must be paid initially. After paying the downpayment balance the transaction should only have the remaining balance as the amounts payable. Only if the remaining balance was paid the transaction will be marked as `paid`.

Edge case:
> what if the customer refused to pay the downpayment set?
> upon cancellation of the initial down payment, the transaction must instanlty be voided.
```
> 2. Upon payment of the **remaining balance** of a transaction, the system shall have the ability to change the payment mode (`cash` or `QrPH`) for the follow-up transaction.
> 3. Transaction modes for `initial payment` and `downpayment` must be individually stated within `Transaction details` (`eye icon`)
> 4. The system must have date and time state to each `Transaction details` (`eye icon`)
> 5. All interfaces must have the ability to view `Transaction details` (`eye icon`)
> 6. Remove `PHP & MySQL Production Artifacts` in the front-end UI. Only the developer must see those artifacts in the codebase.
